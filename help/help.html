Basic step by step

Step 1. Type in a title for the bracket. E.g., Black belt adults.

Step 2. Add the competitors. You can add them by typing the name in and then pressing <enter> or Add. Or you can press the Import File and then select an XLSX or CSV file.

  • When you select a file you will be presented with a view of the data from the file. Click the checkboxes to select the rows and columns that you would like to add.
  • For a seeded match add the competitors in seeded order, highest seed first.

Step 3. Select whether this bracket is for Kata or Kumite.

  • If it is kata you can have the Top 4 or Top 8 as points. Typically you will not have Top 8, but sometimes when there are only 5 competitors you may want to do points for all.
  • If it is kumite you can add a third place bracket by checking the Third Place Match.

Step 4. Check whether this bracket is seeded or unseeded. Typically unseeded for juniors or colored belts.

Step 5. Organize the bracket.

  • Click Shuffle Participants if you would like a random set of pairings.
  • Click on a competitor name and you will get a menu. You can delete, rename or swap positions with another competitor.

Step 6. Save the draw. you can Save the draw to your Google Drive and then Open it again later. You can also Export it to your local machine, and then Import it later.

Once all is good invoke the browser’s print functionality to print the bracket.

Other stuff

  • Why do I have to log into Google all the time? This app doesn't have a backend to securely save your authentication data. Saving on the front end is not secure. To reduce the number of times you need to log in, click the "Clear Bracket" button instead of a page refresh to start a new blank bracket.
  • Why can't I specify the filename? Mostly it's because the Google Picker doesn't easily allow this. The filename is autogenerated by the bracket title and whether it is kata or kumite. A best practice is to complete the kata (or kumite) bracket, press save, switch to kumite (or kata), complete it and then press save again. This will create two files: <title> - kata.bracket and <title> - kumite.bracket. If you use Export with the right web browser you will get a Save As dialog.
